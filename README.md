# Vestigia

**Tracing the work behind the work**

Vestigia is a structured, discipline-agnostic framework that supports students in documenting, reflecting on, and presenting their final-year project work. 

It is available at: **[jesselsookha.github.io/Vestigia](https://jesselsookha.github.io/Vestigia/)**

---

## What Vestigia Is

A final-year project is not defined only by its final submission, but by the trail of thinking, decisions, challenges, and growth that leads to it.

Vestigia exists to help students capture that trail — and then use it.

It is built around two core phases and an optional collective layer:

- **Guide A** — during the project: recording decisions, failures, and reflection as work unfolds
- **Guide B** — after the project: extracting meaning, building narrative, and presenting work professionally
- **Shared Presence** — optional: building a collective group presence for external audiences

---

## What Vestigia Is Not

Vestigia is not a replacement for institutional project manuals, assessment rubrics, or submission systems. It does not mandate tools, formats, or deliverables.

It supports how students think while building.

---

## Relationship to Itan

Vestigia is part of the same open educational ecosystem as [Itan](https://jesselsookha.github.io/Itan/), a broader portfolio development framework for students across all disciplines. Guide B of Vestigia connects directly to Itan — students who want to build a wider professional portfolio around their capstone work will find Itan the natural next step.

---

## For Lecturers and Supervisors

A dedicated **For Lecturers** section on the site provides a full orientation to the framework — what it is, how it works, how to mention it to students, and how it relates to existing assessment structures.

Vestigia sits alongside existing modules without requiring structural change. It does not add assessment burden and does not mandate anything.
